# MBR Motion Hub — Spatial Telemetry Radar & Browser Studio

Official repository for **[tools.mukulmbr.in](https://tools.mukulmbr.in)**.

MBR Motion Hub is a suite of **18 browser-native creator engines** built for 100% local privacy, zero cloud video lag, and browser WebAssembly execution.

---

## 🚀 18 Browser-Native Engines

### 📡 Spatial Radar
1. **Live GPS Telemetry Radar (`/?tool=bus-tracker`)**: Leaflet 3D spatial radar, State RTC selection, registration plate lookup, live tracking URL parser (`YourBus`, `redBus`, `AbhiBus`), and OpenStreetMap Nominatim reverse geocoding.

### 🎬 Video Studio
2. **Prompt Video Editor (`/?tool=prompt-video-editor`)**: Natural language text prompt video editing.
3. **Full Video Editor Studio (`/?tool=video-editor`)**: Multi-track timeline video editor.
4. **Video Trimmer & Cut Studio (`/?tool=video-trimmer`)**: Timestamp trimming & MP4 export.
5. **Video Enhancer & AI Upscaler (`/?tool=video-enhancer`)**: Resolution upscaling & noise reduction.
6. **Camera Director Studio (`/?tool=camera-director`)**: Multi-cam AI scene switching.

### 🎙️ Audio & Speech
7. **Audio Trimmer & Cutter (`/?tool=audio-trimmer`)**: Audio timeline trimming & silence removal.
8. **Audio Track Extractor (`/?tool=audio-extractor`)**: Vocal & background music extraction.
9. **TTS Speech Studio (`/?tool=tts-studio`)**: Web Speech API Text-to-Speech synthesis.

### 🤖 AI & Automation
10. **AI Channel Strategist (`/?tool=channel-strategist`)**: Channel growth AI & DuckDuckGo Instant Answer API research.
11. **AI Subtitle Generator (`/?tool=subtitle-generator`)**: Speech-to-text automated captioning & SRT export.
12. **Auto Video Stitcher (`/?tool=auto-stitcher`)**: Canvas video concatenator.

### 🧰 Media Utilities
13. **Universal Media Downloader (`/?tool=downloader`)**: Instagram Reels, YouTube HD, TikTok (no watermark), Twitter/X MP4/MP3 extractions with iTunes Search API metadata.
14. **GIF Studio Maker (`/?tool=gif-maker`)**: Video & image sequence animated GIF creator.
15. **Thumbnail Generator (`/?tool=thumbnail-generator`)**: Video thumbnail design studio.
16. **Browser Screen Recorder (`/?tool=screen-recorder`)**: Screen, webcam & audio recorder.
17. **Watermark Studio & Remover (`/?tool=watermark-studio`)**: Video inpaint & watermark editor.
18. **Video Compressor Studio (`/?tool=video-compressor`)**: Video compression without quality loss.
